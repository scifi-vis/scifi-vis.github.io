About the Workshop

The SciFi-VIS workshop explores the two-way relationship between science fiction and visualization. We aim to bring together visualization researchers and science fiction creators to investigate how speculative ideas inspire visualization research and how visualization shapes our imagination of the future.

The program includes a keynote, lightning talks, brainstorming sessions, cross-disciplinary critique, and collaborative discussions to identify emerging themes and future research directions.

Call for Participation

If you prefer, a WikiCFP version of this call is available here.

Topics

  • SciFi-inspired visualization concepts and systems
  • Design processes and decisions in SciFi interfaces
  • Speculative and future-oriented visualization design
  • Feasibility and limits of speculative interfaces
  • Evaluation of speculative and imagined visualization systems
  • Physics-based visualizations in SciFi media
  • Shape-changing visualizations in SciFi media
  • References between SciFi and visualization work

Submission Types

  • Short papers and position papers
  • Speculative and future-oriented visualization design
  • Posters, demos, or show-and-tell artifacts
  • Curated SciFi examples (e.g., scene breakdowns + analysis)
  • Speculative designs and artistic explorations
Work in progress and exploratory ideas are welcome!

Submission Length

  • Papers, reports, or documentation: up to 1000 words (approx. 2-4 pages)
  • Other formats: flexible (short is better)

Submission Format

  • VGTC template recommended
  • Up to 4 figures encouraged
  • Ensure proper copyright and attribution
