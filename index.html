About the Workshop

The SciFi-VIS workshop explores the two-way relationship between science fiction and visualization. We aim to bring together visualization researchers and science fiction creators to investigate how speculative ideas inspire visualization research and how visualization shapes our imagination of the future.

The program includes a keynote, lightning talks, brainstorming sessions, cross-disciplinary critique, and collaborative discussions to identify emerging themes and future research directions.

Call for Submissions

We invite submissions in creative and academic formats that explore connections between science fiction and visualization. Contributions may include research ideas, speculative designs, artistic explorations, or position papers.

Join us in shaping future directions at the intersection of imagination and data.

We Are Not The First — Related works linking SciFi to (HCI) Research:

ACM CHI panels chaired by Aaron Marcus, about SciFi writers trying to predict future user interfaces [1992, 1999].
ACE workshop to create a research agenda about SciFi-inspired HCI research [2016].
IEEE VIS VisFutures workshop on to envision futuristic design tools [2020].
Two HCII workshops on HCI/UX in Science-Fiction Movies/TV [2021, 2022].
The blog website scifiinterfaces.com by Christopher Noessel, with extensive analyses of a vast corpus of SciFi movies and TV shows.
