About the Workshop

The SciFi-VIS workshop explores the two-way relationship between science fiction and visualization. We aim to brings together visualization researchers and science fiction creators to investigate how speculative ideas inspire visualization research and how visualization shapes our imagination of the future.

The program includes a keynote, lightning talks, brainstorming sessions, cross-disciplinary critique, and collaborative discussions to identify emerging themes and future research directions.

Call for Submissions

We invite submissions in creative and academic formats that explore connections between science fiction and visualization. Contributions may include research ideas, speculative designs, artistic explorations, or position papers.

Join us in shaping future directions at the intersection of imagination and data.
